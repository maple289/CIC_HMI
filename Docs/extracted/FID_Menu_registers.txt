13.      Menus and Registers
Device settings and readings are organized as registers. The following tables show the registers by name and
number, organized by menu. Registers that can be written are indicated by this symbol: . Others are read-only and
cannot be changed through the MENU or serial communications. Some registers can be accessed only through serial
communications.

         Name          #
